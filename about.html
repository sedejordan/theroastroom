About and privacy

A plain description of what The Roast Room is, how the facts are checked, and how the site handles your data.

What this is

The Roast Room is friendly football banter with receipts. You pick a rival club, hit the button, and read a joke backed by sourced and dated facts. It is built by Fritt as an experiment.

How facts are checked

Every roast has two parts. The joke is an opinion. The facts are sourced and dated. Each evidence item lists the period it refers to, the source with a link, and the date it was accessed. A roast shows the date it was checked, which means a person read each claim against its source on that date. Roasts that fail validation are never shown.

Corrections

If you can show that a roast is wrong, we will fix it or remove it. The most reliable way to reach us is to contact Fritt with the roast and the source that contradicts it.

Privacy

  • The site sets no cookies. The browser remembers which clubs you voted for in localStorage under the key roastroom_voted, on your device only.
  • Suggestions and votes go to a Cloudflare Worker that stores the club name and vote counts, plus a salted hash of your network address for two days for rate limiting. The address itself is never stored.
  • The page fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • The hosting provider keeps standard server logs.
  • The site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Disclaimer

The Roast Room is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any football club, league or broadcaster. Club names are used only to identify the subject and no crests or logos are used.
